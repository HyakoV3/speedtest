        How your data is handled

        When you press Show route or Paste from clipboard and show, or when your browser extension hands over a route, only the public addresses of the routers (never your internal network) are sent to this server, to look up their network and country. They are not saved; only a keyed hash of your own address is kept, for up to an hour, to limit how often this can be asked.

        Privacy Policy

        Route page: when you press Start, your IP address is given to a command on this server that sends test packets to it. The result is shown only to you and is not stored. To limit how often routes are traced, a keyed hash of your address is kept for up to a minute.

        Route page, your route to the server: the text you paste is read in your browser. When you press Show my route, only the public addresses of the routers are sent to this server to look up their network and country; they are not stored, and your internal addresses never leave the browser. To limit how often this can be asked, a keyed hash of your address and the times of your requests are kept for up to an hour.

        This HTML5 speed test server is configured with telemetry enabled.

        What data we collect

        At the end of the test, the following data is collected and stored:

        • Test ID
        • Time of testing
        • Test results (download and upload speed, ping and jitter)
        • IP address
        • ISP information
        • Approximate location (inferred from IP address, not GPS)
        • User agent and browser locale
        • Test log (contains no personal information)

        How we use the data

        Data collected through this service is used to:

        • Allow sharing of test results (sharable image for forums, etc.)
        • To improve the service offered to you (for instance, to detect problems on our side)

        No personal information is disclosed to third parties.

        Your consent

        By starting the test, you consent to the terms of this privacy policy.

        Data removal

        If you want to have your information deleted, you need to provide either the ID of the test or your IP address. This is the only way to identify your data, without this information we won't be able to comply with your request.
